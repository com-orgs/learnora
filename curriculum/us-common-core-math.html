US Common Core Mathematics: Grades 6–8

Use this guide to navigate Learnora's supplementary middle-school maths practice alongside the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. The standards describe mathematical content and practices; they are not a single national lesson plan or a replacement for local school requirements.

What students study across Grades 6–8

  • Grade 6: ratios and rates, fractions and decimals, expressions and equations, geometry, and statistics.
  • Grade 7: proportional relationships, rational-number operations, expressions and equations, geometry, probability and statistics.
  • Grade 8: linear equations and functions, systems, transformations, the Pythagorean Theorem, exponents and bivariate data.

Practise related topics on Learnora

  • Fractions, decimals, percentages and ratios.
  • Grade 7 ratios and proportional relationships, two-step equations and statistics and probability.
  • Grade 8 linear equations, Pythagorean Theorem, linear functions and exponents.

Use the official standards

Read the Common Core State Standards Initiative mathematics standards. Check the relevant state education department and school district for local adoption, sequencing and any additional expectations.

A practical Grade 6–8 topic map

Grade 6 — build number and ratio foundations

Common Core Grade 6 emphasises ratios and proportional relationships, the number system, expressions and equations, geometry, and statistics. Learners develop fraction division, decimal operations, equivalent ratios, unit rates and reasoning about data.

Try Learnora's fractions, decimal operations, ratios and data and graphs lessons.

Grade 7 — apply proportional reasoning

Grade 7 extends proportional relationships and rational-number operations. Students work with percent problems, expressions, equations and inequalities, scale drawings, geometric measurement, probability and sampling.

Practise proportional relationships, rational numbers, equations and inequalities and statistics and probability.

Grade 8 — connect algebra, geometry and functions

Grade 8 develops linear equations and functions, systems of equations, transformations, the Pythagorean Theorem, exponents and scientific notation, and relationships in bivariate data.

Use linear equations, functions and graphs, right triangles and scatter plots for extra practice.

Mathematical practices matter too

Do not study a topic only as a list of procedures. Encourage learners to make sense of problems, explain their reasoning, model real situations, use tools strategically, attend to precision, identify structure and look for repeated reasoning. Ask “why does this work?” as well as “what is the answer?”

How to check local fit

Common Core standards are adopted and implemented through state and local education systems. Check the current state standards, district pacing guide and teacher instructions before using a Learnora page as a lesson sequence. The official standards are the authority; Learnora is a supplementary practice resource.

Scope: Learnora's Grade 6–8 lessons are supplementary practice. This page is a topic guide, not a claim that every lesson has been formally mapped to every Common Core standard.
