UK Maths Curriculum Guide: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland

The UK does not use one identical school curriculum across all four nations. This guide explains the main differences in mathematics terminology and helps learners find related Learnora practice without treating international grade labels as exact equivalents.

England: Key Stage 3

In England, Key Stage 3 covers Years 7–9. The mathematics programme of study includes number, algebra, ratio and proportion, geometry and measures, probability, and statistics. Use Learnora's England KS3 guide for topic links, while checking the learner's school sequence.

Scotland: Curriculum for Excellence

Scotland uses Curriculum for Excellence. The Broad General Education runs from early years to the end of S3 and includes five curriculum levels: Early, First, Second, Third and Fourth. Numeracy and mathematics is one of the curriculum areas. Scottish stage and level labels should not be mapped directly to England's year groups or Learnora's Grade 6–8 labels.

Wales: Curriculum for Wales

Wales organises learning through Areas of Learning and Experience, including Mathematics and Numeracy. The official guidance describes learning progression and supports schools in designing their own curriculum. Use the descriptions of learning and your school's curriculum plan to choose relevant practice; do not assume a direct year-by-year match with England.

Northern Ireland: Key Stage 3

In Northern Ireland, Key Stage 3 is part of post-primary education and covers Years 8, 9 and 10, typically ages 11–14. CCEA provides curriculum and assessment guidance for this system. These year labels differ from England's KS3 Years 7–9.

How to use Learnora across the UK

Learnora organises its core maths lessons by international Grades 6–8. These are supplementary topic resources, not an official UK-wide mapping. Choose the lesson that matches the topic currently being studied, compare it with the appropriate national curriculum and ask the learner's teacher about the expected depth and terminology. Topics include fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, algebra, geometry, statistics and probability.

Important: Learnora is an independent study resource. The England page is England-specific; this UK guide is an orientation page and does not claim full coverage of the four national curricula.
