Study Timer

Study for 25 minutes, then rest for 5. Short, focused sessions with real breaks help you remember more than one long, tired session. Press Start when you are ready — the timer switches between study and break by itself.

Free to use. Works offline. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

How to use it — the 25-minute method

  1. Pick one thing to study, and put everything else away.
  2. Press Start and work until the timer reaches 25:00 — do not check your phone.
  3. Take the 5-minute break: stretch, drink water, rest your eyes. Do not start a game or a video.
  4. Repeat. After four study sessions, take a longer break of 15–30 minutes.
  5. When you finish, try the lesson quiz or the printable worksheet to check what stayed in your memory.

Short sessions work because your brain needs rest to store what you learned. Forcing yourself to study for hours without a break usually means you remember less, not more.
