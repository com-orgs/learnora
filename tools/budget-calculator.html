Worked example

Example: Sara has a fictional monthly amount of $800. She plans $400 for needs, $240 for wants and $160 for savings. That is 50% + 30% + 20% = 100%, and $400 + $240 + $160 = $800, so nothing is left over and nothing is missing. This is exactly the 50 / 30 / 20 guide.

Remember: the 50 / 30 / 20 split is a guide, not a rule. Real families are different — the important habit is to plan before you spend, and to save something every month, even a small amount.
