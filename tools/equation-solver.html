    Worked example

    Question: Solve 3x + 5 = 20.

    Undo the addition first: 3x = 20 − 5 = 15. Then undo the multiplication: x = 15 ÷ 3 = 5. Check: 3 × 5 + 5 = 15 + 5 = 20. Correct.

    Remember: whatever you do to one side of an equation, do the same to the other side — that keeps it balanced.
