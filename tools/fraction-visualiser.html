Fraction Visualiser

A fraction is a part of a whole — but only when the parts are equal. Change the numerator and the denominator below and watch the bar change. The tool also shows the fraction as a decimal, a percentage and in its simplest form.

Free to use. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Worked example

Question: What is 3/4 as a decimal and a percentage?

3 out of 4 equal parts are shaded. As a decimal, 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75. As a percentage, 0.75 × 100 = 75%. So 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.

Remember: the denominator tells you how many equal parts make the whole. The numerator tells you how many of those parts you have. A fraction only describes a fair share when the parts are equal in size.
