    Worked example

    Question: A shop reduces a $60 jacket by 20%. What is the new price?

    First find 20% of 60: 60 × 20 ÷ 100 = 12. Then take it away: 60 − 12 = $48. The jacket costs $48 after the reduction.

    Remember: "percent" means "out of 100", so to find a percentage you always multiply by the percentage and divide by 100.
