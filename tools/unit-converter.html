Unit Converter

Convert length, mass and capacity between metric and common imperial units. The converter goes through a base unit first, so you can see exactly how the answer is worked out.

Free to use. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

    Worked example

    Question: A recipe needs 500 g of flour. How many kilograms is that?

    Convert to the base unit first: 500 g is already in grams. Then divide by 1,000 (grams in a kilogram): 500 ÷ 1,000 = 0.5 kg.

    Key facts to remember: 1 km = 1,000 m · 1 m = 100 cm · 1 kg = 1,000 g · 1 L = 1,000 ml · 1 mile ≈ 1.609 km · 1 foot = 12 inches.
