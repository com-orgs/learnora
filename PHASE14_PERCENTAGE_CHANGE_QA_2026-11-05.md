## Arithmetic checks

### Worked examples and applications
- $40 increased by 10% = $4 increase; new value $44; multiplier 1.10 gives the same result.
- $80 decreased by 20% = $16 decrease; new value $64; multiplier 0.80 gives the same result.
- 30 cm to 36 cm: increase 6/30 = 20%.
- $50 to $40: decrease 10/50 = 20%.
- $60 bill increased by 5% = $3; new bill $63.
- $240 bicycle discounted by 15% = $36; sale price $204.
- 80 to 100 members: increase 20/80 = 25%.
