Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Learnora (“we”, “our” or “the website”) provides educational notes, practice activities, quizzes and printable worksheets. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit learnora-ab2.pages.dev.

1. Information you provide

The current version of Learnora does not offer user accounts, payments, comment areas or contact forms. You can read lessons and use the on-page quizzes without submitting your name, email address or other personal details to Learnora.

2. Technical information and hosting

Learnora is delivered using Cloudflare Pages and Cloudflare’s network services. When you request a page, Cloudflare may process technical information needed to route, deliver and protect the website. This can include your IP address, traffic-routing data, browser or system configuration information, requested URL and security or diagnostic signals. Cloudflare processes information about end users accessing customer websites as described in its Privacy Policy. Learnora does not operate Cloudflare’s network infrastructure or independently control Cloudflare’s operational logs and retention.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The current website experience is designed to work without requiring an account or setting advertising or analytics cookies through Learnora’s own page code. Quiz answers and scores are handled in the page while you use it; they are not submitted to a Learnora account. Your browser or hosting/security services may still use their own technical mechanisms. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

4. Advertising and analytics

Advertising is not currently active on Learnora. The current site does not load Google AdSense advertising code or third-party analytics tags. This policy will be updated before advertising or analytics services are enabled.

If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is introduced, that provider and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertisements. In particular, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s advertising cookies can enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. The relevant vendors, purposes and available choices will be identified here when advertising is activated.

When Google ads are active, visitors can review or change personalised-ad settings through Google Ads Settings. Visitors can also learn about opting out of some third-party vendors’ personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Read How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps for Google’s explanation of its partner-site data use. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Learnora will implement an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework before serving personalised Google ads in those regions. Consent requirements and available ad choices depend on the visitor’s location and applicable law. A consent interface will be provided when advertising technologies that require consent are introduced.

Because some Learnora resources are intended for middle-school grades and may be used by children under 13, the site’s child-directed or mixed-audience status must be assessed before any advertising service is enabled. Where COPPA or other children’s privacy rules apply, Learnora will apply the required child-directed treatment, avoid interest-based advertising to children, and implement any required notice, consent and data-minimisation measures before ad requests are made.

Learnora does not currently run a separate analytics service. If analytics or embedded third-party services are added, this policy will identify them and explain the relevant data processing and choices.

5. Children and students

Learnora publishes general educational material for school-age learners. The website does not currently ask students to create accounts or submit personal information. Parents, guardians and teachers should supervise children’s internet use and should not submit a child’s personal information through any external service linked from the website.

6. External websites

Lessons may eventually link to external educational resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites operated by others. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

7. Data security and retention

We aim to keep the website simple and limit the information it asks visitors to provide. Technical records held by the hosting provider may be retained according to that provider’s operational, security and legal requirements. No internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the website adds features or services. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.

9. Contact

You can contact Learnora at Learnora.site@gmail.com for questions, feedback or corrections. If you email us, we will receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message so we can review and respond. Please do not include passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive personal information. Email correspondence is handled through the email provider and may remain in the mailbox for as long as reasonably needed to respond, keep a record of the conversation or meet applicable obligations.

This page describes the website’s current intended functionality and is general information, not legal advice. It should be reviewed if the site adds advertising, analytics, forms, accounts or other data-processing features.
