GRADES 6–7 · MATHEMATICS

Understanding Percentages

Percentages are used in shopping, scores, surveys, discounts and many everyday situations. Learn what “per cent” means, how percentages connect to fractions and decimals, and how to calculate a percentage of an amount.

Learning time: approximately 30 minutes · Beginner level

What You Will Learn

  • Understand percentages as parts out of 100.
  • Convert common fractions to percentages.
  • Convert percentages to decimals and fractions.
  • Find a percentage of a quantity.
  • Solve simple discount and score problems.

1. What Does Percent Mean?

The word percent means “per hundred” or “out of 100”. The symbol is %.

25100=25%

25% means 25 out of every 100 equal parts.

100% represents one whole. 50% represents one half. 0% represents none of the whole.

2. Connect Percentages and Fractions

To write a percentage as a fraction, put the number over 100 and simplify if possible.

PercentageFraction out of 100Simplified fraction
50%50/1001/2
25%25/1001/4
75%75/1003/4
20%20/1001/5
10%10/1001/10
Example: 40% = 40/100. Divide the numerator and denominator by 20 to get 2/5.

3. Convert a Percentage to a Decimal

To convert a percentage to a decimal, divide by 100. A quick way is to move the decimal point two places to the left.

  • 35% = 0.35
  • 8% = 0.08
  • 125% = 1.25
Why does 8% become 0.08? Eight per cent means 8/100, which is eight hundredths, or 0.08.

4. Convert a Decimal to a Percentage

To convert a decimal to a percentage, multiply by 100. Move the decimal point two places to the right and add the percent symbol.

  • 0.6 = 60%
  • 0.25 = 25%
  • 0.075 = 7.5%
  • 1.2 = 120%
Do not mix up the directions: Percent to decimal means divide by 100. Decimal to percent means multiply by 100.

5. Find a Percentage of an Amount

Convert the percentage to a decimal, then multiply by the amount. For common percentages, you can also use familiar fractions.

Worked example: Find 20% of 60

  1. Convert 20% to a decimal: 0.20.
  2. Multiply: 0.20 × 60 = 12.

Answer: 12.

Another example: Find 15% of 80

Convert 15% to 0.15. Then 0.15 × 80 = 12.

Quick method for 10%: Divide an amount by 10. For example, 10% of 70 is 7. To find 20%, double the 10% amount: 14.

6. Solve Everyday Problems

Example 1: A test score

A student answers 18 questions correctly out of 20. What percentage is correct?

18/20 × 100% = 90%. The student scores 90%.

Example 2: A discount

A bag costs $50 and has a 10% discount. How much money is taken off?

10% of $50 = 0.10 × 50 = $5 discount. The new price is $50 − $5 = $45.

Example 3: A class survey

There are 30 students in a class. If 40% choose football as their favourite sport, how many students is that?

40% of 30 = 0.40 × 30 = 12 students.

7. Common Mistakes to Avoid

Forgetting “out of 100”: 35% means 35/100, not 35 whole units.
Moving the decimal the wrong way: Divide by 100 for percent-to-decimal; multiply by 100 for decimal-to-percent.
Confusing the percentage rate with the money amount: A 10% discount on $50 is $5, not $10.
Assuming every percentage is below 100%: 125% is possible and means 1.25 times the original amount.

11. Lesson Summary

  • Percent means per hundred.
  • To convert a percentage to a decimal, divide by 100.
  • To convert a decimal to a percentage, multiply by 100.
  • To find a percentage of an amount, multiply the amount by the percentage as a decimal.
  • Use fractions, decimals and estimation to check your work.
