Grade 6 Maths Learning Path

Use this page as a guided route through the Grade 6 mathematics topics currently available on Learnora. Each lesson includes clear explanations, worked examples, practice questions, an interactive quiz and a printable worksheet.

Curriculum-neutral learning support · Start with the foundations, then move through the topic groups at your own pace.

Suggested learning order

  1. Build number foundations: understand fractions, equivalent fractions and how to compare them.
  2. Work with fractions: add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions, then connect fractions to ratios.
  3. Develop decimal and percentage skills: understand place value, calculate with decimals and interpret percentages.
  4. Explore geometry and measurement: learn perimeter, area, volume, angles and triangles.
  5. Strengthen number and algebra skills: practise integers, factors, multiples, operations and simple equations.
  6. Interpret information: organise and understand data using graphs.

This is a suggested learning sequence, not an official syllabus. Your school may teach topics in a different order.
