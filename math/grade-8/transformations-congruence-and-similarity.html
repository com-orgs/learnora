Transformations, Congruence and Similarity

Transformations move or resize shapes. They help describe symmetry, congruence and similarity precisely.

Learning time: 20–25 minutes · Level: Developing

Learning goals

  • Understand the key terms and concepts.
  • Apply a reliable method to worked and new problems.
  • Check your reasoning and communicate your answer.

1. Translations

A translation slides every point the same distance in the same direction. Shape, size and orientation stay the same.

2. Reflections and rotations

A reflection flips a shape across a line called the mirror line. A rotation turns a shape around a fixed centre by a specified angle and direction.

3. Congruent figures

Congruent figures have the same shape and size. One can be mapped onto the other using rigid transformations: translations, rotations and reflections.

4. Similar figures

Similar figures have the same shape but may have different sizes. Corresponding angles are equal and corresponding side lengths are in the same ratio.

5. Scale factors

The scale factor is image length divided by original length. A factor greater than 1 enlarges; a factor between 0 and 1 reduces. Areas scale by the square of the factor.

Worked examples

  1. A point (2, 1) translated by (3, −2) becomes (5, −1).
  2. A 90° anticlockwise rotation about the origin maps (x, y) to (−y, x).
  3. A side of 4 cm enlarged by scale factor 2.5 becomes 10 cm.
  4. If similar shapes have side scale factor 3, their area scale factor is 9.

Independent practice

Attempt each problem before checking the worked answer.

  1. Translate (1, 4) by (2, −3). Answer: (3, 1).
  2. Reflect (−3, 5) in the y-axis. Answer: (3, 5).
  3. Rotate (2, 1) 90° anticlockwise about the origin. Answer: (−1, 2).
  4. Are two squares with side lengths 3 cm and 7 cm congruent? Answer: No; they are similar but different sizes.
  5. A 6 cm side is enlarged by factor 4. Answer: 24 cm.
  6. A shape is reduced from 10 cm to 5 cm. Answer: scale factor 0.5.
  7. Similar figures have side scale factor 2. What is area scale factor? Answer: 4.
  8. If corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides proportional, what is the relationship? Answer: Similarity.

Knowledge check

Choose one answer for each question, then check your answers for explanations.

1. Which transformation slides a shape without turning it?

2. Congruent figures have…

3. A 5 cm side enlarged by scale factor 3 becomes…

4. For similar shapes, corresponding angles are…

5. If side scale factor is 4, the area scale factor is…
