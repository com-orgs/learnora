# Learnora — Competitor Set & Keyword Strategy

**Research date:** 3 November 2026  
**Site:** https://learnora-ab2.pages.dev/  
**Scope:** International English education resources, with the strongest current coverage in Grades 6–8  
**Markets:** Global English discovery first; curriculum-specific pages for the US, UK, Canada and Australia  
**Source status:** Competitor set is a manually curated market/SEO research set, not a Semrush-exported list of 50 confirmed ranking competitors.

## Executive summary

Learnora's strongest current search opportunity is not to compete head-on for broad, highly competitive terms such as `math worksheets` with a new generic page. It is to build useful, well-linked grade-and-topic pages around specific learning needs, then support them with genuinely useful quizzes, worked examples and printable practice where the site actually offers those formats.

The current sitemap contains 104 URLs. The most developed topic cluster is middle-school mathematics (Grades 6–8), with additional English, Science, international curriculum, Computer Science, AI Literacy and Financial Literacy sections.

**Verified Keyword Tool metric:** Google, Global / Worldwide, English — `math worksheets`: 90,500 monthly searches, trend 0%, competition score 0.31. This is one cached keyword row only. It is a broad head term and is not evidence that a new Learnora page can rank for it.

**Keyword discovery coverage:** 12 seed searches returned 49–394 autocomplete ideas each depending on topic. These are suggestion counts, not monthly search volumes. In this guest access run, metrics were not available for the individual suggestions. Do not convert suggestion counts into search demand.

**Semrush limitation:** the connected Semrush account returned `no_api_units` for both Competitive Research and Organic Research. Therefore this report does not claim Semrush-confirmed competitor overlap, competitor traffic, competitor rankings, keyword gaps, or keyword search volumes for competitor domains. Re-run those reports when Semrush API units are available.

### How to use this competitor set

- **Tier A — direct search competitors:** Khan Academy, IXL, Education.com, CK-12, BBC Bitesize, worksheet libraries, ReadWorks, CommonLit, Quill and science lesson sites. Compare page titles, content depth, internal links, examples, practice and printable formats.
- **Tier B — specialist benchmarks:** PhET, NASA, Code.org, Scratch, NGPF and Practical Money Skills. Learn from their clarity and teaching quality; do not copy their product model.
- **Tier C — adjacent study/quiz platforms:** Quizlet, Quizizz, Kahoot, Brainly and Knowt. Compare quiz intent and study workflows, not just lesson-page SEO.

## 4. Recommended keyword priorities

### Priority 1 — strengthen existing pages first

1. Grade 6–8 maths hubs and topic pages.
2. Fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, equations and word problems.
3. Grade 7–8 science topic pages.
4. Middle-school grammar, reading comprehension and writing.
5. Worksheet landing pages, but only for downloadable/printable resources that actually exist.

Reason: Learnora already has a substantial cluster of pages in these areas. Better internal linking, clearer titles and stronger lesson usefulness can improve topical coherence without creating thin new pages.

### Priority 2 — expand topical differentiation

1. Computer Science for beginners.
2. AI literacy for students.
3. Financial literacy for students.
4. Country-specific curriculum resources.

These can differentiate Learnora from maths-only worksheet competitors, but need strong, accurate, age-appropriate lessons and clear sources.

### Priority 3 — avoid until evidence improves

- Broad generic targets such as `education`, `math`, `science` or `worksheets` on individual lesson pages.
- Country-specific exam terms without exact curriculum alignment.
- `PDF`, `printable`, `answer key` phrases when the page does not provide that format.
- AI-generated keyword pages with little original teaching value.
- Duplicating the same keyword across multiple URLs.

## 5. On-page implementation rules

1. Keep each URL's primary keyword aligned with its actual lesson and grade.
2. Put the primary phrase naturally in the title, H1 and opening paragraph; do not repeat it unnaturally.
3. Write a unique meta description that describes the actual lesson and benefit.
4. Use descriptive internal links between grade hubs, related lessons, worksheets and curriculum guides.
5. Add FAQ questions only when the page provides a useful, accurate answer; FAQ schema is not a guaranteed rich result.
6. Add `worksheet`, `quiz`, `answers` or `PDF` to metadata only when that feature is visibly present and functional.
7. Do not create thin doorway pages for US/UK/Canada/Australia. Country pages must explain real curriculum differences.
8. Keep child privacy and accessibility requirements in place. Do not add behavioural profiling or student-level tracking for SEO.

## 4 November 2026 — free autocomplete refresh (no paid metrics)

A limited Google autocomplete sample was collected with the free guest Keyword Tool for English-language searches in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. Five suggestion seeds and three question-type seeds were tested. The guest tool returned suggestion text but no cached volume, trend, competition or CPC metrics for these terms; these are intent clues, not measured search-volume opportunities.

### Useful intent themes
- Grade 6 math worksheets and practice worksheets.
- Grade 6 maths questions, tests and answer explanations.
- Fraction word problems, equivalent fractions, adding/subtracting, multiplying and dividing fractions.
- Questions such as “what math should 6th graders know?”, “how to divide fractions in 6th grade”, and “how to multiply fractions in 6th grade”.
- Australia question suggestions included “what should a grade 6 student know in maths”.

### Regional noise and exclusions
- UK and Australia samples unexpectedly returned terms such as “MCO”, “ATP”, “Green Shoots” and “aiming for Grade 6 maths”, which appear mismatched to Learnora's intended international middle-school audience and may reflect regional/provider noise.
- Do not create pages or change metadata for these terms without independent validation against the target country's curriculum and actual SERPs.
- Do not add “PDF”, “answer key” or exam-paper promises unless the exact printable/answer format is present and functional on the destination page.

### Implementation decision
- Use suggestions to shape useful FAQs and examples on existing Grade 6 Maths pages, not to create thin new keyword pages.
- Keep the 41-URL map as the target map; avoid cannibalising existing fraction lessons.
- Search volume, difficulty and competitor ranking gaps remain unverified until an authorised source returns metrics. Semrush API reports remain unavailable due to insufficient API units.
