## Source verification and suitability
- Paragraph writing: Purdue OWL's “On Paragraphs” supports unity, coherence, topic sentences and adequate development. British Council LearnEnglish's A2–B1 “Organising Your Writing” gives an accessible example of using paragraphs to separate topics and order information. Purdue is supplementary, not a required middle-school text.
- Parts of speech: Purdue OWL's Parts of Speech Overview covers word classes with examples; British Council's English Grammar Reference offers accessible explanations of nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs and other grammar areas.
- Punctuation and capitalization: Purdue OWL's punctuation overview and sentence punctuation patterns cover commas, quotation marks, apostrophes, semicolons and clause joining. Purdue's capitalization guide and British Council's B1–B2 capital letters/apostrophes page support capitalization and apostrophe rules.
- Reading comprehension: Reading Rockets' Basics: Reading Comprehension and Inferencing pages support prior knowledge, inference, evidence and self-monitoring. Its Texas Education Agency-based “Strategies that Promote Comprehension” resource supports main ideas, supporting details, summaries and before/during/after reading strategies.
- Sentence types and structure: British Council's elementary clause-structure page supports subjects, verb phrases, objects, complements and adverbials. Purdue OWL supports fragments, run-ons and sentence punctuation. Its sentence-structure PDF describes simple, compound, complex and compound-complex patterns and is labelled optional advanced extension.
- Verb tenses and agreement: British Council's beginner Present Simple and Past Simple pages support basic forms and irregular verbs. Purdue OWL's verb-tense reference gives a technical explanation of tense and auxiliaries, including future meaning; its subject/verb agreement guide covers compound subjects, intervening phrases and indefinite pronouns. The source section makes the technical status of the Purdue references clear.
- Vocabulary: Reading Rockets' Vocabulary overview and “Vocabulary: Instructional Guidelines and Classroom Examples” support word learning through context, dictionaries, affixes, base words and roots. The direct link to the more specific Content Area Vocabulary page returned an access restriction, so it was replaced with the accessible topic overview.
